How it works

Five steps, from unit register to compliant token

An illustrative Australian wholesale fund, structured as a unit trust, follows the process Tokeniser documents in its own Knowledge Hub.

  1. 1

    Legal review

    The trust deed is checked to confirm it allows ownership records on chain. The existing licence covers it, with no separate tokenisation licence.

  2. 2

    Set the rules

    On Tokeniser, the manager sets who can invest, foreign ownership limits and lock-up periods. The rules are written into the token.

  3. 3

    Verify investors

    Identity, anti-money laundering screening and investor classification come first. Until then, no tokens can reach a wallet.

  4. 4

    Issue on Redbelly

    Tokens for each investor's units are issued on Redbelly Network into verified wallets. The register is live from the first token.

  5. 5

    Checked transfers

    Every transfer is checked against the rules first. Break a rule and it is rejected. Pass and it settles in seconds.

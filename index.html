The five steps

An illustrative Australian wholesale fund, structured as a unit trust, following the process Tokeniser documents in its Knowledge Hub.

  1. 1
    Legal reviewThe trust deed is checked to confirm it allows ownership records on chain. The manager's existing licence covers it. There is no separate tokenisation licence.
  2. 2
    Set the rulesOn Tokeniser, the manager sets who can invest, foreign ownership limits and lock-up periods. The rules are written into the token itself.
  3. 3
    Verify investorsIdentity, anti-money laundering screening and investor classification come first. Until then, no tokens can reach their wallet.
  4. 4
    Issue on RedbellyTokens for their units are issued on Redbelly Network into verified wallets. The register is live from the first token.
  5. 5
    TransfersEvery transfer is checked against the rules first. Break a rule and it is rejected. Pass and it settles in seconds.

## Voice specific terms

The model's voice list (`VOICES.md` in the same repository) marks a small number of Japanese and French voices with an extra CC BY attribution requirement for their training audio. The `af_heart` voice used here carries no such marker. Its SHA-256 prefix `0ab5709b` matches the value listed for `af_heart` in that table.

## Why Apache 2.0 covers this use

Apache License 2.0 grants a perpetual, worldwide, royalty free licence to use, reproduce and distribute the work, with no restriction on commercial use. Audio generated by running the model is output of the software, not a copy of the model, so the film carries no attribution requirement. This register records the source anyway, for transparency.

## Terms

The brand kit page states:

> These assets are intended to preserve the image of Redbelly in the different formats of use of its visual identity.

The brand manual sets these rules, which the film follows:

- Keep the separation and proportions between the isotype and the logotype, and keep the brand space clear.
- Do not alter proportions, spaces and dimensions. Avoid low contrast. Do not use versions not in the manual or files.

In the film the negative logo sits on Redbelly navy (`#142530`) and Tokeniser ink (`#343740`), both dark grounds, with clear space on all sides and a height of at least 70 px on a 1080 px frame.

## Colours and type taken from the kit

- Main brand colours: `#FF5050` red, `#283A46` grey.
- Secondary colours: `#FA423C`, `#142530`, `#848B91`, `#DCDCDC` (shown as `#D9D9D9` on the brand kit web page), `#ECEBEC`.
